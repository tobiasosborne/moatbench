## Actual field production

Liebherr's [Mount Thorley Warkworth report](https://www-assets.liebherr.com/media/bu-media/lhbu-min/brochures/job-reports/job-report-r-9800-yancoal-mine_final.pdf), page 4, reports 16.25 million bank cubic metres moved by an R 9800 over May 2024–April 2025, with an average dig rate of 2,763 bcm/hour. This is manufacturer-reported overburden removal at Yancoal's mine. It is external evidence, not an independently replicated MoatBench trial or a sandcastle result.

The source specifies a customised 48 m³ backhoe bucket; this differs from the face-shovel specification above. BCM measures material in its original position, before excavation. The hourly denominator includes direct operating time and inherent delays. A material-production rate is not a moat-completion score.

Sources are linked rather than redistributed because their photographs and publications are not public domain.
